## Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. **However, our App does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to external servers.**

### Camera Permission
Our App requires access to your device's **Camera**. This permission is strictly used to scan QR codes and barcodes locally on your device. The images captured by the camera are processed directly on your device and are **never** saved, uploaded, or shared with us or any third parties.

### Wi-Fi and Network Permissions
Our App requests permissions to access and change Wi-Fi states. This is solely to enable the feature that allows you to automatically connect to a Wi-Fi network when scanning a valid Wi-Fi QR code. We do not track or collect your network information.

### Local Storage
If you use the feature to save a generated QR code, the App will request permission to write to your device's local storage (Gallery). This data remains strictly on your device.

## Log Data and Analytics
